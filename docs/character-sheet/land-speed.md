## Land speed

The owner decided on 2026-11-17 that the sheet computes **land speed**: the race's base speed, its typed bonuses (Fast Movement, Fleet, haste, longstrider), and the reduction for medium or heavy armor. Carried load stays a Note and encumbrance stays out of scope (PRD #251's "encumbrance" exclusion); climb, swim, fly and burrow speeds are not computed.

### Statistic

- **Target.** `speed.land` is a Modifier target, in feet ([Modifiers](modifiers.md#targets)). Its bonuses stack as any target's do ([Stacking](stacking.md)): two enhancement bonuses (haste's +30, longstrider's +10) don't stack, untyped bonuses do.
- **Base.** A race gives its base speed as a `base` Modifier on `speed.land` (Foundry races' `speeds.land`). `base` doesn't stack, so one race speed applies.
- **Conditions.** A bonus that holds only in light or medium armor carries `armorAtMost` ([Modifiers](modifiers.md)); in heavier armor it is suppressed with its reason, such as "Lost while wearing Full plate (heavy armor).", and in body armor of no known category too, "(its armor category is unknown)" (root, 2026-11-18: only armor known to qualify grants it). The condition `armorAtMost: 'none'` (rotation-5) is lost in any body armor and in nothing else: any body armor suppresses it, whatever its category ("Lost while wearing Leather armor (light armor).", or "(its armor category is unknown)" for armor saved without one), and it holds with a shield and under any Condition. `unarmored` is not that condition: it also drops with a shield or while helpless. No committed row carries `none`; whether the monk's Fast Movement should is open (see [What carries a speed](#what-carries-a-speed)).
- **No base, no speed.** Without a `base` Modifier, the sheet shows no land speed (`derivedStatistics.landSpeed` is absent), so a race no release has given a speed shows none rather than 0 or a bonus alone.

### Armor reduction

`deriveLandSpeed({ statistic, wornArmor, armorTrainingTier, exemption })` (`src/lib/character-sheet-speed.ts`) gives `derivedStatistics.landSpeed` from the `speed.land` statistic:

- **The reduction.** While the heaviest armor worn (an active armor item; never a shield or a Spell Effect) is medium or heavy, the total is reduced after its bonuses: 20 ft. to 15 ft., 30 ft. to 20 ft., 40 ft. to 30 ft., `speed − 5 · floor(speed / 15)`. The PRD capture's gnoll, base 30 ft., in +1 scale mail has Speed 20 ft. (`monsterCodex/gnolls.html:558`); Foundry gives the same rule (`ActorPF.getReducedMovementSpeed`) and its armor items list "Speed (30 ft.) 20 ft.; Speed (20 ft.) 15 ft.". Bonuses come first: "Apply this bonus before modifying the barbarian's speed because of any load carried or armor worn" (CRB `barbarian.html:381`). The reduction is a line in the breakdown under the armor's name, such as "Chainmail (medium armor) −10".
- **Armor Training** lifts it: "a fighter can also move at his normal speed while wearing medium armor. At 7th level, a fighter can move at his normal speed while wearing heavy armor" (PRD `coreRulebook/classes/fighter.html:384`), so tier 1 (3rd level) in medium armor and tier 2 (7th level) in heavy armor. The tier is `armorTrainingTier`'s ([Weapons and armor](weapons-and-armor.md)).
- **Slow and Steady** lifts it: a dwarf's "speed is never modified by armor or encumbrance" (PRD `advancedRaceGuide/coreRaces/dwarves.html:362`), while the dwarf's Slow and Steady racial trait (`pf1-content/JsvoElxHBPdle8s8`, rule key `slow-and-steady`) is active.
- A lifted reduction is listed as suppressed with its reason, such as "Armor Training 1: moves at normal speed in medium armor."
- A Temporary Effect's bonus and armor leave the permanent projection, as for every statistic.

The sheet shows it as "Speed" beside Initiative in Offense, such as "20 ft.", with the breakdown every Offense figure opens (`offense-block.tsx`).

Tests: `src/lib/character-sheet-speed.test.ts` (30 to 20 and 20 to 15 in medium and heavy armor, Fast Movement in medium and lost in heavy, enhancement bonuses, Armor Training tiers, Slow and Steady, a shield, no base speed, both projections), `src/lib/character-sheet-armor-none.test.ts` (`armorAtMost: 'none'` with a shield, in light armor, in armor of no category or `other`, while helpless; `light` and `medium` lost in armor of no category or `other` and beside light armor, as before in light, medium and no armor, `unarmored` unchanged; every schema that lists the condition), `convex/characterSheetSpeedMaxima.integration.test.ts` (a race copy without a speed reads as before; the saved sheet for owner, member and outsider), `convex/characterSheetArmorNoneDice.integration.test.ts` (`none` on the saved sheet, in light armor and in armor saved with no category; a personal adjustment and an unknown limit refused), `src/components/character-sheet/offense-block.test.tsx`.

### What carries a speed

Since stats-d2 the catalog's rows do ([importer](../catalog-import/README.md), "Speeds" and "Land speed changes"):

- **Every race** gives its base speed as its `base` Modifier, from Foundry's `speeds.land`; the launch and party races agree with the PRD capture (Human, Elf, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Aasimar, Orc and Tiefling 30 ft.; Dwarf, Gnome and Halfling 20 ft.). A Racial Trait's own speed change stays prose, because the race's speed is the base.
- **Features, feats, items and Spell Effects** whose Foundry change targets `landSpeed`: an untyped bonus for Foundry's `base` (Travel domain +10, Cinder Dance +10), the enhancement bonus of longstrider (+10) and expeditious retreat (+30), and so on. Each was read against its pinned body and PCGen line: a feature's bonus is unconditional, and a Spell Effect's or buff's holds while that entry is active.
- **Situational bonuses stay out** of the ordinary total (PRD #251: "Situation-dependent contributions stay out of ordinary totals"), reported with their reason, when no Modifier condition states the situation: Dance of the Blades' +10 needs a wielded metal weapon, so a Human with it and no weapon moves 30 ft.; Travel as One's −10 stands for the Travel Domain's replaced increase, so it takes nothing off (beside the Travel Domain, that domain's +10 stays: a replaced part of a feature is not modelled); the Cybernetic Leg's +10 needs both legs, and one item is one leg. A withholding row that stops matching its record fails the import ([curation](../catalog-import/curation.md#reviewed-unsupported-changes)).
- **Reviewed change replacements** ([curation](../catalog-import/curation.md#reviewed-change-replacements)). Checked: Fast Movement (barbarian, unchained barbarian, bloodrager), +10 untyped while the armor is at most medium; Fleet, +5 untyped while the armor is at most light, each copy adding (the feat's effects stack; checked on the stats-d2 round 3 reviews). Drafted, applying nothing until the curation review checks it: the monk's Fast Movement, an enhancement bonus of `10 × floor(monk level / 3)` while `unarmored` (lost in armor, with a shield or while helpless). Which condition the row should carry is a rules question for that review, and the pinned text has two passages that differ: the Fast Movement paragraph names armor and load ("A monk in armor or carrying a medium or heavy load loses this extra speed", PRD `coreRulebook/classes/monk.html:405`, Unchained `unchained/classes/monk.html:439`), while the Weapon and Armor Proficiency paragraph lists using a shield among the ways a monk loses fast movement ("When wearing armor, using a shield, or carrying a medium or heavy load, a monk loses his AC bonus, as well as his fast movement and flurry of blows abilities", Core `monk.html:382`, Unchained `monk.html:404`). Since rotation-5 a row may also carry `armorAtMost: 'none'`, lost in body armor alone; this slice settles neither reading.
- **Not yet:** haste and the other bonuses capped by the base speed (no formula variable reads the base speed), the oracle revelation Fleet and Shadow's Speed (their `@class.level` has no class), and the Duergar's Slow and Steady (not keyed to `slow-and-steady`).

So a party sheet with a written race shows a Speed, Fast Movement in it: characters 3 and 5 show 20 ft., 4 and 10 30 ft. in medium armor, 7 40 ft., 8, 9, 11 and 13 30 ft. Checking Fleet's row moved no party number (nobody has Fleet), and checking the monk's would move none (its Fast Movement is +0 at Monk 1).

Tests: [`land-speed-d2.test.ts`](../../tests/catalog/land-speed-d2.test.ts) imports the pinned races, features, feats, armor and Spell Effects and resolves the party's sheets by character number, committed and with the drafted replacement checked in memory, a dwarf in full plate, the monk's Fast Movement lost in armor, two enhancement bonuses that don't stack, Fleet's copies stacking, the three situational bonuses left out, and rotation-5's rules on these rows: what the generic condition `armorAtMost: 'none'` does on the monk's row, replaced in memory (it holds with a shield and any body armor loses it; the committed row, drafted with `unarmored`, applies nothing) and the checked Fast Movement and Fleet lost in armor of no known category.
