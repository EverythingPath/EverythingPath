# Archetype structures: two mechanical Alchemist archetypes (#659)

A #716 structure batch by class (batch name `archetype-alchemist-1`; discriminator 659, the owning curation issue: [#659](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/659) owns the alchemist Archetypes). Since `archetype-select-2` the import emits 42 Alchemist archetypes behind `--pcgen`, every row held on its structure gate or omitted behind a held feature. This batch writes the structure records of two of them, 7 rows, in `scripts/catalog/reviewed-curation.json` `structures`: one root record and five feature records. They were written `drafted`, and the record step checked all six on one captured review (receipt `t3-review-2b6976d45da76e15a25be9006681ab6f5d45743c6d0543e2df22eadad92ba301`, [The record step](#the-record-step)), so all 7 rows are admitted. The rows are preview-only: no release scope names them (owner decision 2026-11-10).

Sources: PCGen at `25523dbc` (`roleplaying_game/ultimate_combat/uc_abilities_class.lst`, `roleplaying_game/monster_codex/mc_abilities_class.lst`) and the PRD capture `prd-2026-10-20` (`ultimateCombat/classArchetypes/alchemist.html` and `monsterCodex/goblins.html`, both added to the pinned store byte-identical by this batch). Each `sourceSha256` is the one the structure report of a `--pcgen` preview on the base `06cefcf0` gives. No owner or feature record, and no `.MOD` line naming one, carries a Product Identity flag.

## The batch

Eleven of the 42 have no review item but `stackingGuard`, PCGen defects and owned features by the selection's count; on this base the mapper also says where an archetype's own text disagrees with it, and the Beastmorph now carries such an item, so ten remain. ## What the sheet computes

Nothing new. No record gives a number: every feature keeps `modifiers: []`, and the root record re-affirms the mapper's rows. Of the replaced Alchemist rows none carries a Modifier or rule value; Poison Resistance, which carries the one Modifier among these rows (+2, +4 and +6 on saves against poison), is not replaced. At Alchemist 1, 4 and 8 the archetypes change which rows show and which go dormant, and the Winged Marauder its class skills: a ragechemist has the Discoveries row at 1 (the mapper adds it there; its text is a list and grants nothing), Rage Mutagen in place of Poison Use at 4, and also Sturdy Rage in place of Swift Poisoning at 8; a winged marauder has Flying Beast Tamer in place of Mutagen from 1, and the mapper's class skills, which move the +3 class-skill bonus on a skill with at least one rank: an Alchemist 1 with one rank in each and ability modifiers 0 has Acrobatics, Handle Animal and Ride 1 and Disable Device, Sleight of Hand and Use Magic Device 4, a winged marauder 4 and 1 (the batch test reads it on the sheet). From 10th level a ragechemist keeps Poison Resistance's +6 against poison where an alchemist is immune. What stays prose, each situational or nothing the sheet computes:

- Rage Mutagen's, Sturdy Rage's and Lumbering Rage's bonuses, each only while his rage mutagen is in effect, which the sheet does not hold (the Mutagen row carries no Modifier), and the penalties on Will saves and to Intelligence (with Lumbering Rage's to Dexterity) that each turn he takes damage under it may give him, which stack and end 1 hour after the mutagen ends (PRD `ultimateCombat/classArchetypes/alchemist.html:367`): situational, and not ended with the mutagen.
- Discoveries' list, which grants nothing.
- Flying Beast Tamer's dire bat or giant vulture companion: no companion rule keys on the feature or the alchemist.

No per-day maximum is bound.

## The record step

The promotion review (run `t3-review-2b6976d45da76e15a25be9006681ab6f5d45743c6d0543e2df22eadad92ba301`, reviewer gpt-6.2-sol, LAND) confirmed all six records, and the record step checked them: each now names that review in `receipt`, which binds its fields and `resolves` (the report copy is in `scripts/catalog/review-reports/`). So the gate settles the Ragechemist's defect and every owned feature, and all 7 rows of the two archetypes are admitted under #227 on their declared books, 5 features and both roots; none stays held or omitted. No record binds a number, so no sheet total moves with the records; the Winged Marauder's class skills are the mapper's and move the class-skill bonus as [What the sheet computes](#what-the-sheet-computes) says. The rows stay preview-only: no release scope gains one.

The nine left out have no record and keep their state: none is admitted. Each waits for what the table under [Left out](#left-out) names: Beastmorph for the mapper to read its text's replacement; Construct Rider, Promethean Alchemist and Inspired Chemist for a record field that carries a granted feat or a feat in place of a discovery (and the Construct Rider's extracts per day); Deep Bomber for one that carries extracts known and a text that names its replacements; Bogborn Alchemist, Homunculist, Blazing Torchbearer and Sacrament Alchemist for an allowed text that names their replacements, without the scanning error or PCGen's note (the capture has no page for any of them).

The Winged Marauder is for goblins (PRD `monsterCodex/goblins.html:371`); the imported Archetype model has no race eligibility field, so nothing restricts it to a goblin (a #716 importer follow-up, queued by root).
