# Archetype structures: two mechanical Paladin archetypes (#684)

The fourth #716 structure batch by class (batch name `archetype-paladin-1`; discriminator 684, the owning curation issue: [#684](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/684) owns the paladin Archetypes). Since `archetype-select-2` the import emits 45 Paladin archetypes behind `--pcgen`, every row held on its structure gate or omitted behind a held feature. This batch writes the structure records of two of them, 10 rows, in `scripts/catalog/reviewed-curation.json` `structures`, written `drafted`: eight feature records and one root record. The record step checked all nine on a captured review (run `t3-review-d752c96b6febaf800c1a94be65ddb766b91ba8f7e1a9fe3d1ca0145485b3dc43`), so all 10 rows are admitted ([the record step](#the-record-step)). The rows are preview-only: no release scope names them (owner decision 2026-11-10).

Sources: PCGen at `25523dbc` (`roleplaying_game/advanced_players_guide/apg_abilities_class.lst`, `roleplaying_game/ultimate_combat/uc_abilities_class.lst`) and the PRD capture `prd-2026-10-20` (`advancedPlayersGuide/coreClasses/paladin.html`, already in the pinned store; `ultimateCombat/classArchetypes/paladin.html`, now in it). Each `sourceSha256` is the one the structure report of a `--pcgen` preview on the base `3f5d8131` gives (the root's, `archetypeSourceSha256` of its line and `.MOD` line). No owner or feature record, and no `.MOD` line naming one, carries a Product Identity flag.

## The batch

Eleven of the 45 are mechanical by the selection's count, which screens both the mapper's items (none but `stackingGuard` and owned features) and the text (no PCGen substitution token, conditioned fragment or Product Identity flag). Two of them pass the further checks: an allowed text names every row the mapper replaces, at the level the Paladin gains it; no bound text carries a PCGen substitution token (a variable such as `%1`, or a `%%` escape emitted unread) or a conditioned fragment, while a literal percent sign is ordinary text (Shared Defense binds the PRD's "25%" where PCGen writes its escape `25%%`); no replacement leaves a dice value or a per-day maximum wrong; and every feat, slot, proficiency or spell a feature gives is bound (fix r1, root's bar of 2026-11-18: a description alone is honest only when taking the archetype leaves nothing on the sheet wrong).

- **Tactical Acumen's slots.** Its record binds `grantsSlots: [{ kind: 'feat', count: 1, featTypes: ['Teamwork'] }]` (the Foundry tag of the Inquisitor's reviewed teamwork slot, `scripts/catalog/pcgen/schedule-slots.ts`), prerequisites checked as the text says, so a Holy Tactician has one Teamwork slot at 3rd to 6th level, two at 7th, three at 11th, four at 15th and five at 19th, and no other bonus feat slot.

## The record step

The review confirmed all nine records. The record step marked them `checked` with that run as their `receipt`.

As committed, all 10 rows are admitted under #227: Divine Defender and its two features on PZO1115, Holy Tactician and its six features on PZO1118. None stays held or omitted. Each archetype replaces its reviewed rows, whole, and adds its features at their levels. Holy Tactician's root record applies, so Tactical Acumen is added at 3rd, 7th, 11th, 15th and 19th level, and its record's slot applies to each of those rows: a Holy Tactician has one Teamwork feat slot at 3rd, two at 7th, three at 11th, four at 15th and five at 19th, and no other bonus feat slot. No other emitted key changes state, and no release scope gains a row. The nine archetypes left out below keep their state: none has a record, and each stays held or omitted behind its held features.

## What the sheet computes

No record gives a Modifier or a rule value. Holy Tactician's records give the teamwork feat slots above. Of the replaced rows none carries a landed value (Smite Evil, Mercy and Divine Bond carry none yet), and a replaced row is dormant. At Paladin 1, 4 and 8:

- **Holy Tactician**'s Weal's Champion replaces Smite Evil whole, so any per-day maximum Smite Evil later carries goes with it; Weal's Champion's own uses then need a rule-value record (a follow-up).
- **Lay on Hands** keeps its dice (2d6 at 4, 4d6 at 8) in both; Shared Defense spends its uses. Channel Positive Energy keeps its dice in both.
- **Mercies:** Divine Defender has none (Mercy is replaced whole).
- Saves, AC, attacks and skills don't change.

What stays prose, each situational or something the sheet doesn't compute: Weal's Champion's uses and bonuses against evil targets; Divine Defender's armor bond, its bonuses and uses; Shared Defense's sacred bonuses to allies; the allies' benefits of Battlefield Presence, Guide the Battle, Weal's Wrath and Masterful Presence.

- The committed records have exactly these fields, items and hashes, and all nine carry the record step's receipt. Each description is read from the pinned PRD page. The nine left-out archetypes have no record.
- As committed, every gate passes and the records apply. All 10 rows are admitted under #227 on their declared books (PZO1115, PZO1118), and no other key changes. Each archetype replaces exactly its reviewed rows and adds its features at their levels.
- Drafted in memory, no emitted key changes state against the overlay without the records. Every feature is held on structure, and the roots are omitted behind their features.
- On the sheet (the archetype prepared as the release converter prepares it, on the Paladin's emitted rows), each archetype gives one slot of its feature's types per row reached and no other bonus feat slot at every level 1-20: Holy Tactician 0 at 2nd, 1 at 3rd, 2 at 7th … 5 at 19th; drafted in memory, none.
