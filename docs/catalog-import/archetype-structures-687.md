# Archetype structures: seven mechanical Rogue archetypes (#687)

A #716 structure batch by class (batch name `archetype-rogue-1`; discriminator 687, the owning curation issue: [#687](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/687) owns the rogue Archetypes). Since `archetype-select-2` the import emits 59 Rogue archetypes behind `--pcgen`, every row held on its structure gate or omitted behind a held feature. This batch writes the structure records of seven of them, 24 rows, in `scripts/catalog/reviewed-curation.json` `structures`: seventeen feature records, sixteen of them `description` only and Rake's Smile's `description` with its `modifiers`. They were written `drafted`, and the record step checked all seventeen on one captured review (receipt `t3-review-384299b8c0469c9a27696e1a51fba65e604a512253222f841a507e74a10aef65`, [The record step](#the-record-step)), so all 24 rows are admitted. The rows are preview-only: no release scope names them (owner decision 2026-11-10).

Sources: PCGen at `25523dbc` (`roleplaying_game/advanced_players_guide/apg_abilities_class.lst`, `roleplaying_game/occult_adventures/oa_abilities_class.lst`, `campaign_setting/pathfinder_society_field_guide/pfsfg_abilities_class.lst`) and the PRD capture `prd-2026-10-20` (`advancedPlayersGuide/coreClasses/rogue.html`, added to the pinned store byte-identical by this batch; `occultAdventures/archetypes/other.html`, already there). The capture has no Pathfinder Society Field Guide page, so the Scroll Scoundrel's descriptions are PCGen's DESC. Each `sourceSha256` is the one the structure report of a `--pcgen` preview on the base `3f5d8131` gives. No owner or feature record, and no `.MOD` line naming one, carries a Product Identity flag.

## The batch

Eighteen of the 59 have no review item but `stackingGuard` and owned features by the selection's count. Seven of them pass the further checks: an allowed text names every row the mapper replaces, at the level the Rogue gains it, no text carries a `%`, a conditioned fragment or a scanning error, no feature is shared with an owner its text does not fit, and taking the archetype leaves nothing on the sheet wrong (root decision 2026-11-18: a feature is `description` only when its effect is situational, needs a choice the sheet does not hold, or touches nothing the sheet computes; an unconditional number, an entitlement or a changed rule value is bound or the archetype is left out). The Scout's two features are also the Ninja's Scout archetype's (`uc_abilities_class_apg.lst:5`, `.MOD` lines below it, loaded only with the Advanced Player's Guide); their texts and levels fit both owners, uncanny dodge at 4 and improved uncanny dodge at 8.

- **The roots.** None has a review item, so none has a record. Each was still checked row by row against its features' "This ability replaces …" in the PRD (the Scroll Scoundrel's in PCGen) and the base Rogue's levels (CRB `coreRulebook/classes/rogue.html:387-410`), since their features' records admit them with the mapper's rows. The Rogue class and every replaced row are admitted, so no root stays omitted.
- **The descriptions.** Each APG and OA description is the PRD's text for the feature, without its heading, with links and emphasis as plain text, entities as characters and a feature's paragraphs joined by a space; the Scroll Scoundrel's five are PCGen's DESC verbatim (`pfsfg_abilities_class.lst:94-98`). After flattening, four differ from PCGen's DESC and take the PRD's: Stab and Grab (PCGen "-5"), Scout's Charge and Skirmisher (PCGen's are second-person rewrites without the level and the "replaces" sentence) and Brutal Beating (PCGen "itself - only"). Haunting Presences is two PRD paragraphs and equals PCGen's DESC once they are joined. The other twelve equal PCGen's DESC.
- **Sneak attack and rogue talents.** None of the seven changes sneak attack's dice or progression; Bravado's Blade, Brutal Beating, Scout's Charge, Skirmisher, Stab and Grab and Spot Weakness use it in prose. No talent is granted or restricted beyond the replaced rows; the PRD's talent lists are suggestions.
- **Finding.** The Scroll Scoundrel's own description (PCGen `pfsfg_abilities_class.lst:89`, the root) has the scanning error "conf licts". The root has no review item and no record, so its emitted description keeps PCGen's text, error included, as every unrecorded root does. The five feature descriptions are their own DESC lines (`:94-98`), which are clean, so Sirocco Fury's disposition for a faulty feature description (no text bound, [archetype-structures-679.md](archetype-structures-679.md)) does not apply to them, and their records stand as reviewed (promotion review, follow-up 1).

## What the sheet computes

One number: Rake's Smile's checked record binds two Modifiers, morale on `skill.blf` and `skill.dip`, `floor(@classLevel.rogue / 3)` ("At 3rd level, a rake gains a +1 morale bonus on Bluff and Diplomacy checks. This bonus increases by +1 for every 3 levels beyond 3rd.", PRD `advancedPlayersGuide/coreClasses/rogue.html:434`; PCGen `BONUS:SKILL|Bluff,Diplomacy|(RogueLVL/3)`, `apg_abilities_class.lst:2975`, the same amount with no type): +1 at Rogue 3 to 5, +2 at 6 to 8, from the feature's 3rd level. Every other feature keeps `modifiers: []`, and no root has a record. Of the replaced Rogue rows only Trap Sense carries Modifiers, its two situational "vs. traps" lines on Reflex and AC, which go dormant with the row (+1 at 3, +2 at 6 to 8); Trapfinding, Uncanny Dodge, Improved Uncanny Dodge and the rogue talent rows carry no Modifier or slot. At Rogue 1, 4 and 8 the archetypes change which rows show and which go dormant, and a rake's Bluff and Diplomacy rise by 0, +1 and +2. What stays prose is situational, a foe's, or nothing the sheet computes, and each record's rationale says which:

- Dim the Lights' bonus (only in dim light or darkness); Fast Talker's (only on unlikely lies, and on the Diplomacy check that adjusts a starting attitude); Adaptive Learning's (only against effects already saved against); Pay It Back's (only against foes who attacked him).
- Bravado's Blade, Brutal Beating, Scout's Charge, Skirmisher, Stab and Grab, Spot Weakness: on a sneak attack, a charge, a move or one target; the sheet adds no sneak attack to weapon damage.
- Measure the Mark, Follow Up, Frightening, False Sensitivity, Haunting Presences, Elusive Gambit: opposed checks, rerolls, a foe's condition or narrative.

## Tests

- The committed records have exactly these fields, items and hashes, and each is checked under the record step's receipt. Each APG and OA description is read from the pinned PRD page. The eleven left-out archetypes and their shared features have no record.
- As committed, every gate passes and the records apply. All 24 rows are admitted under #227 on their declared books (PZO1115, PZO1132, PZO9235); no other key changes against the overlay without the records. Each archetype replaces exactly its reviewed rows and adds its features at their levels.
- Drafted in memory (each record without its reviewer, date and receipt, as the batch wrote it), no emitted key changes state: every feature is held on structure, and the roots are omitted behind their features.
- On the sheet (pinned Rogue and Rake rows, ability scores 10, no ranks), a rake's Bluff and Diplomacy are 0, +1 and +2 at Rogue 1, 4 and 8, unarmored and in a chain shirt alike (whose −2 reaches Acrobatics only); a Rogue without the archetype, and the records drafted in memory, give 0.

## The record step

The promotion review (run `t3-review-384299b8c0469c9a27696e1a51fba65e604a512253222f841a507e74a10aef65`, reviewer gpt-6.2-sol, LAND) confirmed all seventeen records, and the record step checked them: each now names that review in `receipt`, which binds its fields and `resolves` (the report copy is in `scripts/catalog/review-reports/`). So the gate settles every feature, and all 24 rows of the seven archetypes are admitted under #227 on their declared books, 17 features and seven roots; none stays held or omitted. A rake's sheet now shows Rake's Smile's bound morale bonus on Bluff and Diplomacy, `floor(@classLevel.rogue / 3)`: +1 at Rogue 3 to 5, +2 at 6 to 8, and so on to +6 at 18 to 20. The rows stay preview-only: no release scope gains one.

The eleven left out have no record and keep their state: none is admitted. Each waits for what the table under [Left out](#left-out) names: Acrobat for an armor-check waiver the sheet models, Sniper for a range-penalty rule (both code); Burglar, Trapsmith and Poisoner for a per-owner text of a shared feature; Escapologist for a serves-as record; Guild Agent and Master of Disguise for a text without the conditioned fragment, Heister for one that names its replacements, Numerian Scavenger for one without PCGen's note and scanning error (no allowed source has any of these whole); Carnivalist for a mapper that reads its own talents and sneak attack.
