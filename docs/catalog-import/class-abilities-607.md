## #607 S1: the bonus on the sheet (2026-11-14)

Sol (AI curator) for the owner. Sources: the pinned body, PRD `coreRulebook/classes/monk.html:383-384` and `unchained/classes/monk.html:406-407`, PCGen `cr_abilities_class.lst:1090` (Monk AC Tracker: `max(WIS,0)` and `min((MonkACLVL)/4,5)`, on AC and CMD) and `:1095`.

- **Computed now.** Two [reviewed change replacements](curation.md#reviewed-change-replacements) replace Foundry's two changes (target `ac` and `cmd`, untyped), whose `if(and(lt(@shield.type …)))` gate is outside the grammar, with `max(0, @abilities.wis.mod) + floor(max(@classLevel.monk, @classLevel.monkUnchained) / 4)`. It imports as `max(0, @ability.wis.mod) + floor(max(@classLevel.monk, @classLevel.monkUnchained) / 4)` on `ac` and `cmd`, untyped. The Wisdom **bonus** "(if any)" is never negative. The untyped AC bonus lands on `ac.other`, so AC, touch AC and flat-footed AC all take it, as the rule says ("even against touch attacks or when the monk is flat-footed"); the `cmd` Modifier gives CMD and flat-footed CMD (an untyped AC bonus doesn't reach CMD, so nothing counts twice). The formula names the class tags because the body has no `system.class`, so the importer's `@class.level` rewrite can't apply. Both classes grant the feature, and the import gives Monk (Unchained) its own tag, `monkUnchained`, and no relationship to the Monk, so its levels count only under its own tag; the formula reads both. It takes the higher of the two, not their sum, so it stays right if that relationship is ever supplied (the levels would then count under both). A tag the sheet has no levels in reads 0, and no other class counts. The ten increments keep no Modifiers. A monk has at most 20 monk levels, so +5 is the most it gives.
- **Not computed: the loss conditions.** The bonus is lost in armor, with a shield, under a medium or heavy load, or when immobilized or helpless. A Modifier can't test any of these, so the bonus is always on. The record's Note gives the conditions in the rulebook's words and tells the player to switch the row off while one applies: "He loses these bonuses when he is immobilized or helpless, when he wears any armor, when he carries a shield, or when he carries a medium or heavy load. The sheet does not check this: switch this row off while any of these applies." Until switched off, a monk in armor sees AC and CMD too high. Testing armor and shields in code would rotate the calculation identity; it waits for a later rotation (S2).
- **Scaled Fist.** The Scaled Fist uses Charisma where the monk uses Wisdom for this bonus (Draconic Might). The archetype's root is held on the owner's Product Identity question, and S1 doesn't model the swap: a Scaled Fist's sheet shows the Wisdom version until the archetype is admitted. The stopgap: switch the AC Bonus row off, and add a personal adjustment, untyped, on AC and on CMD, of the Charisma modifier (minimum 0) plus the level part (monk levels / 4, rounded down): `max(0, @ability.cha.mod) + floor(max(@classLevel.monk, @classLevel.monkUnchained) / 4)`. Switching the row off removes both parts of the bonus, so the adjustment carries both; it reads both tags, so it is right for an Unchained monk too.
- **Re-binding.** A re-bind of every assessment over the full-corpus `--pcgen` previews before and after the batch moves one binding: this entry's own (content only; its evidence, notices, reviewer and date stay wave A's). No other assessment's content covers the entry. The entry's fixture copies are updated: this batch's `binding-preview.json` and #355's ([`feats-355/binding-preview.json`](../../tests/fixtures/feats-355/binding-preview.json)). The protected contexts of #350 to #354 and #530 to #533 store the key and its historical definition hashes, not a copy of the entry, so the content change doesn't re-bind them.
- **Preview.** Before and after, only this entry changes (its two Modifiers, its Note, its two `changes` issues gone); no row changes admission.
- **Sheet test.** [`monk-ac-bonus-607.test.ts`](../../tests/catalog/monk-ac-bonus-607.test.ts) resolves the imported entry through `calculateCharacterSheet` on AC, touch AC, flat-footed AC, CMD and flat-footed CMD: Monk 4 with Wisdom 14 gets +3, with Wisdom 8 +1, Monk 1 with Wisdom 12 +1, Monk 20 with Wisdom 10 +5, Monk (Unchained) 4 with Wisdom 14 +3, Monk (Unchained) 20 with Wisdom 10 +5, Monk 1 / Fighter 4 with Wisdom 12 +1; a Fighter gets nothing, and with the row switched off each bonus is gone. The classes' tags and relationship are the import's own, re-extracted from the pinned class bodies: Monk (Unchained) carries `monkUnchained` and no relationship to the Monk. With that relationship added, Monk (Unchained) 4 with Wisdom 14 still gets +3 and 8 with Wisdom 10 +2, not double; a Fighter who somehow holds the feature gets the Wisdom bonus alone, and no case raises a formula warning.
