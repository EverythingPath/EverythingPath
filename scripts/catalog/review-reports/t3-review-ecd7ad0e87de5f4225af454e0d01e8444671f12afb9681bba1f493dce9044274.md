## Values at every Magus level

The base is `max(1, floor(Magus level / 2)) + permanent Intelligence modifier`. The PRD gives the same half-level component and minimum and says “The pool refreshes once per day when the magus prepares his spells.” Preparing spells controls the refresh. The maximum is an unconditional capacity in points, with no choice of ability or pool. PCGen explicitly floors the half-level and uses `INT`. Below Magus 1 the entry is absent, so there is no number. The table uses Intelligence 16 (+3) and shows feat copies hypothetically applied to the base. The feat has no separate maximum or character-level prerequisite above its pool prerequisite.
