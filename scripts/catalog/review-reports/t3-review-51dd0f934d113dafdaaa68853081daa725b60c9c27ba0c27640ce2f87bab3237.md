## Findings

1. **Hard. Guide Terrain Bond, Spirit Bond and Warden Terrain Bond remove the Ranger's animal-companion entitlement through Hunter's Bond.** A Character with an existing animal-companion link sourced from a Ranger Class Level can apply one of these archetypes and keep the unsupported Ranger contribution. An in-memory probe gave the same result for all three and for the base Ranger: the class source was entitled, counted, and classified as `rangerCompanion`. The resulting contribution is Ranger level minus 3. The replacement rows accurately record Hunter's Bond becoming dormant. The companion calculation also needs to honor that loss of entitlement. [Guide Terrain Bond](/home/andreas/.t3/worktrees/everythingpath/t3-af04936e-973907aaaac9c9d7/scripts/catalog/reviewed-curation.json:325326), [Spirit Bond](/home/andreas/.t3/worktrees/everythingpath/t3-af04936e-973907aaaac9c9d7/scripts/catalog/reviewed-curation.json:325356), [Warden Terrain Bond](/home/andreas/.t3/worktrees/everythingpath/t3-af04936e-973907aaaac9c9d7/scripts/catalog/reviewed-curation.json:325467), [Core Rulebook](/home/andreas/repos/everythingpath/sources/prd-2026-10-20/pages/coreRulebook/classes/ranger.html:451), [companion entitlement](/home/andreas/.t3/worktrees/everythingpath/t3-af04936e-973907aaaac9c9d7/convex/lib/companionRelationshipGraph.ts:634), [source kind](/home/andreas/.t3/worktrees/everythingpath/t3-af04936e-973907aaaac9c9d7/convex/lib/companionRelationshipGraph.ts:361), [effective level](/home/andreas/.t3/worktrees/everythingpath/t3-af04936e-973907aaaac9c9d7/src/lib/character-sheet-companion-sources.ts:668).
2. **Hard. Spirit Bond adds ordinary Ranger spell castings to the daily allowance.** It grants one casting of an eligible Ranger spell without preparation at 4th level, rising to two at 8th and increasing by one every four levels. The sheet calculates spells per day and the record supplies no contribution for these flexible castings. The separate augury spell-like ability can stay in prose. [Spirit Bond record](/home/andreas/.t3/worktrees/everythingpath/t3-af04936e-973907aaaac9c9d7/scripts/catalog/reviewed-curation.json:325356), [APG source](/home/andreas/.t3/worktrees/everythingpath/t3-af04936e-973907aaaac9c9d7/tests/fixtures/pinned/prd/pages/advancedPlayersGuide/coreClasses/ranger.html:456), [spells per day](/home/andreas/.t3/worktrees/everythingpath/t3-af04936e-973907aaaac9c9d7/docs/character-sheet/spellcasting.md:94).
