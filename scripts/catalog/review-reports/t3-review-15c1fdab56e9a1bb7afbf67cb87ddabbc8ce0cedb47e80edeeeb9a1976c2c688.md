## Values at Bard levels 1–20

Bardic Performance is 4 plus the permanent Charisma modifier at Bard 1, then adds 2 per additional Bard level. PCGen gives `2 + CHA + 2 × BardicPerformanceLVL` and assigns Bard level to that progression for the Bard grant. The APG adds one round per counting Class Level with the selected reward. The resulting maximum is unconditional in rounds per day, with no rounding step or stated minimum. Below Bard 1 the sheet gives no maximum, including if Extra Performance is present.
